01  /  Why Blue

Most agencies are handed a fragment and asked to make it shine. We ask what the fragment is part of.

BLUE was built on a refusal: no brief is only about the brief. A launch is a culture question. A rebrand is an internal one. A media plan is arithmetic with people on the end of it.

So we work at the width of the whole problem — strategy, story, craft and spend in one room, answerable to a single idea. It takes longer to start and considerably less time to finish, because nothing has to be rescued in the handover. There isn't one.

02  /  What We Do

SIX WAYS
TO BE SEEN

Six disciplines. One of them is filled in.

Positioning, architecture, naming, and the arguments that hold them up. We look for the true thing you can own — then make it defensible enough to survive a change of marketing director.

One idea, sized for every place it has to live — a six-second pre-roll, a shop window, the side of a building. If it only works in the deck, it isn't the idea yet.

Art direction, design systems, tone of voice, and the standards that keep them intact across a hundred touchpoints. The part where taste stops being a matter of opinion.

Film, stills, motion and sound, made in-house at the volume the feed actually eats — without letting the craft drop to meet the calendar.

Planning, buying, and the honest reporting afterwards. Paid, owned and earned budgeted as one number, because the audience never knew they were separate.

Sites, stores and products, built to carry the brand rather than apologise for it — fast, accessible, and still recognisably yours.

04  /  How We Think

LOOK. FRAME.
MAKE. MEASURE.

Four moves, in that order,
every single time.

01

LOOK

The first weeks are spent refusing to solve anything. Category, culture, competitors, and the customer's actual Tuesday. At this range nothing is legible yet, and pretending otherwise is how briefs go wrong.

02

FRAME

Pull back until one sentence holds everything in shot. If the strategy needs a deck to survive the lift, it isn't a strategy yet — it's a mood.

03

MAKE

Ideas are cheap until they are built. Design, film and media are made alongside each other, so nothing thins out on the way to production.

04

MEASURE

We agree the numbers before the work runs, and we show you the ones that didn't move. Only from here can you see what you were looking at.
